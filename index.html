About Herbal Spa

Herbal Spa is a wellness spa brand with over 10 years of experience, operating in Da Nang and Hoi An. It is positioned as a trusted wellness destination for both local customers and international travelers seeking relaxation and holistic healing experiences.

The spa offers signature treatments such as Four-Hands Harmony, Art of Eight Hands, and Herbal Aroma Journey. These therapies combine traditional Vietnamese healing practices with modern spa techniques, using natural herbal ingredients.

Herbal Spa is designed as a private and tranquil wellness environment, featuring a nature-inspired aesthetic influenced by Northwest Vietnamese culture. Each treatment room is created to ensure privacy, calmness, and deep relaxation.

With a team of professionally trained therapists with over 5 years of experience, Herbal Spa delivers personalized treatments focused on comfort, care, and full-body relaxation.
